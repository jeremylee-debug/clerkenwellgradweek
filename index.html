About the Event

Where Regional Talent
Meets Clerkenwell

A platform for the next generation of interior and product designers

Clerkenwell Grad Week (CGW) is an annual event held every July, running alongside New Designers at the Business Design Centre — a moment when London is alive with emerging design talent.

For 2026, Egger London Showroom will host 10 students from Teesside University to showcase their work, run presentations, network, tour showrooms, and meet professional contacts across the Clerkenwell district.

A collaboration between regional design universities, Interior Educators charity, and Clerkenwell Showrooms — the social enterprise supporting UK interior and product design graduates.

Programme

Three Days, One District,
Endless Opportunity

CGW 2026 spans three days of showcasing, touring, networking, and celebrating design excellence across the Clerkenwell district.

Day One
1 July

Showcase & Awards

  • Egger London Showroom Students arrive to represent their work, run presentations, and network with professionals.
  • New Designers @ BDC Business Design Centre opens — visit and explore emerging student talent.
Day Two
2 July

Showroom Tour Day

  • Guided Clerkenwell Tour Students tour showrooms including Tarkett Atelier, Frem Group, Shaw Contract, MagScapes and RAK Ceramics.
  • IE Awards Display Winning entries on display at Miller Knoll for the duration of the week.
  • Clerkenwell Showrooms - Terrace The CGW reception for: Students, Showroom host and Tour Partners.
Day Three
3 July

IE Awards Viewing

  • New Designers @ BDC Final days of New Designers at Business Design Centre.
Teesside University
  • Teesside University Students visit IE Awards at Miller Knoll then return to Teesside by train.

Join Clerkenwell Grad Week 2026

Whether you're a showroom looking to host students, a university seeking to showcase graduates, or an industry professional wanting to connect — CGW 2026 has a place for you.

Clerkenwell Grad Week grows each year, with more showrooms, more students, and more activities celebrating regional design excellence in the heart of the Clerkenwell district.

  • Host students and showcase your brand to the next generation
  • Feature in CGW communications and social media coverage
  • Network with regional universities and design educators
  • Be part of a growing movement championing British design talent
